Account & privacy

Your Microsoft sign-in stays in the Microsoft flow.

Launcher sign-in is used to authenticate Minecraft Java access. Sword Client does not ask you to send your Microsoft password to the project.

Authentication

Microsoft sign-in is handled through the system browser. Xbox and Minecraft services complete the checks needed to launch the game.

Local launcher data

The launcher keeps account/session data and game files on your device in its Sword Client application folder. See the privacy page for details.
