Privacy

How Sword Client handles sign-in data

This page describes Microsoft sign-in and local data for the Sword Client Windows launcher.

Microsoft sign-in

When you choose to sign in, the launcher opens Microsoft’s sign-in page in your system browser. Sword Client does not ask you to type or send your Microsoft password to the project. Microsoft, Xbox, and Minecraft services process authentication and account checks so the launcher can determine whether the account can access Minecraft Java Edition.

Data stored on your device

The launcher stores account/session information and Minecraft files locally in the Sword Client folder under your Windows application data directory. You can use the launcher’s sign-out control to clear its saved account information. Removing the local Sword Client folder also removes local launcher and game data.

Information sent to services

During sign-in and launch, authentication tokens and the requests needed to verify your Xbox and Minecraft account are sent to Microsoft, Xbox, and Minecraft services. Those services handle the data under their own privacy terms.

Analytics and advertising

This project website does not include analytics or advertising scripts in this page.
